# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, founders, and partners in Pakistan and the Gulf (UAE, KSA, Qatar, Bahrain) who open this on a laptop in a bright office and decide, in about ninety seconds, whether Mughees Dewan can be trusted with a regulated, money-moving, or operationally messy product. Secondary: recruiters screening for Senior/Lead Product, TPM, and platform roles.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that gets Mughees into conversations for Senior/Lead Product, TPM, and platform roles in Pakistan, the Gulf, or remote. Success is a WhatsApp, email, or Calendly from someone who understood the work, not a compliment on the design.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, consequential, composed. Trust first; cleverness second. The voice is precise about numbers and honest about caveats. It should feel like a person who has sat in rooms where a wrong system costs a licence, a season, or a repayment.

## Anti-references

- Dark SaaS landing pages with particle motion and metric-card grids (the default "PM portfolio")
- Navy-and-gold Gulf finance cliché, Pakistan-flag green, mosque/desert ornament
- Editorial-magazine AI portfolios (Instrument Serif, tracked labels, italic drop caps, no photography)
- Generic Inter / card-grid / gradient-text developer templates
- Inflated "I 10x'd" copy, fake metrics, or hiding the 45-farmer vs 40,000-user distinction

## Design Principles

1. **Show the decision, not the title.** Each case is a problem, a choice, and what it produced. Titles are secondary.
2. **Earn the number.** Caveats stay visible. PKR 150M, 45 farmers, 3,069 acres, 40,000 users, and July 2026 go-live are different facts.
3. **Face first for this market.** Photography carries trust in PK and Gulf hiring; a tiny circular crop is not enough.
4. **WhatsApp is the door.** Gulf and Pakistani hiring happens on WhatsApp. The primary CTA is a conversation, not a form.
5. **Institutional, not startup-costume.** The work is regulators, banks, and field operations. The site should feel like a gazette or a banker's card, not a YC launch.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, keyboard navigation, `prefers-reduced-motion`, semantic landmarks, and alt text that describes the person and the diagrams' claims. English primary; a single Urdu name mark is identity, not a second language version. Do not rely on hover for any action.
